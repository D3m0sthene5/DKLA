# DKLA v2.5 explanation review

Reviewed October 11, 2026. Every main case now has one individually authored plain-English introduction: 392 in total. The 391 existing introductions were compared with their effective canonical rule and **complete canonical Holding** (40,801 words of Holding prose). This pass rewrote 108 of those introductions and added the missing Swiss comparison after reading its complete seed account and all ten supplied supplement pages 129–138. The 1988 general principles were kept separate from the 2024 facts that begin on page 130.

These comparisons actually read the designated sections. They are not a fresh complete-opinion audit of 392 judicial decisions. Eleven ambiguous cases additionally received complete canonical Material Facts and Reasoning comparisons; the earlier timeline pass had read seven divided decisions' complete Reasoning and the complete saved Szukhent and Anderson main-case briefs. Source fingerprints record provenance and detect drift, rather than implying the rest of every brief was reread.

The principal corrections address meaning, scope and posture:

- Pennoyer, Finley, Aldinger, Livingston, Hoffman, Humphrey's Executor, Debs, Brand X and other older decisions are identified as historical where later cases or legislation change the rule. Hoffman now distinguishes the current all-parties-consent transfer route. Wyoming's 2025 noncompete statute is distinguished from Hassler's 2022 redrafting decision; New York's current online dating-service exceptions are distinguished from the 2005 Great Expectations case.
- Calder requires defendant-created contacts expressly aimed at the forum; knowing where the plaintiff feels harm is insufficient. Goodyear's contacts-based general jurisdiction is distinguished from Mallory's statutory-consent route and reserved Commerce Clause challenge. Mullane requires reasonably calculated notice, rather than guaranteed actual receipt.
- Hadges's party-motion safe harbor is distinguished from court-initiated sanctions. Whitman's self-restraint sentence was corrected: an agency cannot cure an invalid delegation by promising restraint. Kisor concerns deference, rather than all persuasive consideration of an agency's views. Chadha requires presentment, which does not invariably require a presidential signature.
- Divided reasoning, circuit-specific tests, state-specific contract rules, preliminary injunctions, stays, pleading sufficiency, and remands are distinguished from nationwide rules, final liability findings, class certification and completed later proceedings. Arnett and Mathews distinguish a prior evidentiary hearing from all prior process. The Swiss comparison retains reduction of a penalty and its punitive function without importing the domestic invalidation rule.

## Inventory and reproducible checks

`tools/v25/explanation-inventory.json` inventories default effective text after canonical sections, rule additions and exact-value plain/audit overlays. It includes case introductions, case rule/notes, other node explanations, embedded course treatments, connection explanations and timeline prose. It does **not** mark all inventoried text substantively reviewed. User-authored saved edits, dictionary definitions and authority-library source text are separate surfaces. The root noncase and timeline review ledgers record additional work with their own scope.

`tools/v25/explanation-audit.json` contains one review record for every introduction, the exact revised text, any prior text, the concrete reason for each correction, sections actually read, canonical locators/hashes, inherited citations/PDF pinpoints and separately identified independent sources. The Swiss record explicitly identifies a supplied machine translation rather than a freshly obtained foreign original.

```text
python tools/v25/explanation_inventory.py --write
python tools/v25/explanation_inventory.py --check
python tools/v25/generated_rule_connections_check.py
node tools/v25/generated_rule_connections_check.cjs
python tools/v25/explanation_inventory.py --pack takeaway 0 20
python tools/v25/explanation_inventory.py --pack noncase 0 20 --node-kind Rule
python tools/v25/explanation_inventory.py --pack course-treatment 0 20
```

`--check` fails on stale inventory, changed reviewed text, changed compared sections or source fingerprints, missing coverage, or missing referenced primary checks. It requires the saved baseline and canonical text inputs, without needing the large source PDFs. Regenerating an inventory is a mechanical operation; it does not refresh the review ledger or turn an inherited claim into fresh verification. A failed review check needs deliberate inspection and an affected-record update after reading the change.

Neither this pass nor the timeline pass rewrote full briefs, Cold Call narratives or narrative fingerprints. Complete current-law certification and comprehensive negative-treatment searches across all jurisdictions remain outside these targeted comparisons.
