## Contract and dispute reported

A student agreed to receive distance classes over six years. The payment terms described were a thirty-franc enrollment charge and thirty-five monthly installments of forty francs. Nine months after the course began, he sought to terminate. The chapter reports that the institution demanded the remaining twenty-six monthly installments and that the lower court refused to order their payment. These are the author's reported facts, not independently checked findings from an available opinion. (Assigned 200.)

The Cour de cassation is reported to have quashed that refusal and held that the institution could recover the entire fee balance. The reported twenty-six installments should not be turned into twenty-six years, six unpaid annual fees, or a judicially awarded additional damages figure. The chapter does not state why the student withdrew, whether instruction continued or was tendered, what contractual termination terms existed, or what defenses he pleaded. (Assigned 200.)
