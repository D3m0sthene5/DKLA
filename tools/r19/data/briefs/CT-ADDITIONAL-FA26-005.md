# Dijon computer-equipment replacement decision — assigned account, source-limited
Cour d’appel de Dijon, 19 September 1989, Juris-Data no. 1989-049228 | Contracts

## Source and identity

The assigned comparative-law chapter reports a Dijon Court of Appeal decision ordering replacement of nonconforming computer equipment and identifies it in footnote 43. The full French judgment has not been recovered. This account is confined to that short secondary assigned description, not a full judicial opinion or an English translation of one. Its locators refer to Rowan's printed page 202, physical supplement page 117.

## Facts and remedy reported

The chapter says a vendor originally supplied computer equipment that failed to conform to the contractual specification. The court ordered performance of the sale contract by requiring replacement. It does not identify the parties, equipment model, contractual terms, defect, price, replacement availability, trial decision, or competing arguments. A replacement order is the result reported by the chapter; no additional damages, rescission, costs, timetable or disposition can be verified from it. (Assigned 202 & n. 43.)

## Assigned doctrinal use

The author uses this decision to illustrate specific enforcement of obligations to deliver goods. The surrounding explanation contrasts enforcement of the promised performance with an award that merely pays for substitute performance. This is the chapter author's doctrinal framing. Without the actual judgment, it cannot establish that the Dijon court expressly considered and rejected a mitigation argument, an impossibility defense, a supervision objection, or a proportionality test. (Assigned 202.)

Footnote 43 also cites a Colmar decision of October 18, 1972 and further literature. Those citations are additional authorities, not proof that the Dijon and Colmar courts heard the same dispute. The next building and airline examples concern other decisions; their facts and outcomes are excluded from this account. (Assigned 202 & nn. 43–47.)

## Limits and outstanding work

The whole assigned page and linked footnote were read in native text and the original page image. Retrieval has not produced a complete actual reproduction of Juris-Data 1989-049228. Judicial reasons, precise procedural result, panel composition and any notes or separate writings remain unverified.
