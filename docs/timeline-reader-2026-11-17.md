# Timeline reader and Dottie improvements — 17 November 2026

The timeline reader now separates the question, main takeaway, case sequence,
later developments, and supporting evidence. Course browsing includes topic/case
search, descriptive cards, and a separate complete case index. Case-brief
sequences expand vertically, rather than requiring sideways reading. Introductions
and source details are keyboard-operable disclosures, and readers can jump to the
entries or return to the top. The three complete course indexes have their own
case search. All existing case identities, original readings, and saved-work
formats are retained.

## Content scope

All 96 doctrine timelines and 685 entries received an editorial review. The dated
redesign register contains 1,706 guarded operations: 96 titles, 57 questions,
96 guides, 78 syntheses, 685 concrete effect labels, 415 core notes, 250 context
expansions, five later-treatment notes, one exact date, and 23 source-link groups.
These are editing operations, not 1,706 independently discovered legal errors.

The Contracts pass corrects overstatements about open prices after Sun Printing,
reviving a rejected offer, a buyer's knowledge of a concealed defect, peculiar
knowledge and reliance disclaimers, and Spearin/Lenawee comparisons. The Civil
Procedure pass qualifies heightened pleading, jurisdiction and finality,
deposition use, court-questioned jurisdictional amounts, and New Jersey additur
following Orientale. The LRS pass clarifies statutory procedure after Bi-Metallic,
pretermination safeguards in Mathews, and Atlas Roofing's particular agency and
public-rights setting. Every correction retains its recorded source and review
depth. An independent review supported the 14 targeted correction groups and
refined the Mathews source pinpoint.

The course-specific coverage ledgers and independent review are preserved under
`tools/content-audit/evidence/timeline-redesign/`. The original correction register
explicitly links its successor so integrity checks follow later changes without
rewriting earlier evidence.

This pass is not a fresh claim-by-claim verification of every opinion. Most
rewrites improve existing source-linked teaching accounts; newly checked primary
passages are distinguished in the register. The three complete case chronologies
receive the reader redesign, complete-inventory checks, and search; their 1,469
summaries were not all independently re-audited in this pass.

## Dottie regression

The question “What litigation could be brought against the FAA after the recent
ground stop debacle?” exposed a blanket instruction to abstain from questions
about real legal matters. Dottie now requests supported conditional legal analysis
with prerequisites and limits. It separates uncertainty about the incident from
the available legal framework and can ask for a decisive fact. Current-event
research receives an as-of date and a bounded six-paragraph allowance. Narrow
filter changes preserve legitimate alternatives and incident clarification while
retaining checks against guessed cases/statutes, unsupported substitutions, and
fabricated citations. No storage or collection policy changes were made.
