## Reconstruction from the readings
* Sullivan, C&M 13–18. A disappointed patient can establish breach of a promised result even if she fails to prove negligent treatment. The third operation, additional suffering and worsened appearance sit differently from the normal costs of the two contemplated operations. Waivers allowed the award to stand without an exclusive choice of expectation or reliance. The problem on 18–19 varies fees, pain and performance. DKLA's problem-interests asks the student to compute the measures, preserving the exercise. [363 Mass. 579, 580–82, 587–89](https://law.justia.com/cases/massachusetts/supreme-court/volumes/363/363mass579.html)
* Naval Institute, C&M 21–26. A defendant's profits can sometimes help estimate the plaintiff's lost business. Other profits, including those from readers who would wait for paperback, cannot be awarded on that rationale. Independent evidence showed some hardcover sales were displaced. The uncertainty went to amount. [936 F.2d 692, 696–98](https://law.justia.com/cases/federal/appellate-courts/F2/936/692/401988/)
* Morris, C&M 26–29. The horse's value to the trainer and the limits of an ordinary substitute supported an equitable order. Cashing the check for undisputed wages did not discharge the separate promise to deliver the horse. K-34/b → f captures the first point. Its alternative → g works for an adequately replaceable chattel. [225 Ark. 1019, 1020–22](https://law.justia.com/cases/arkansas/supreme-court/1956/5-854-0.html)
* White, C&M 30–34. Lost use of the water caused actual inconvenience, including taking the children elsewhere to bathe. The Supreme Court reinstated $10 for that harm and denied the punitive award on the case pleaded. The court's opinion controls the contradictory publisher annotation. [37 Wis. 2d 285, 288–92](https://law.justia.com/cases/wisconsin/supreme-court/1967/37-wis-2d-285-13.html)
* C&M 35–36 and 44–48. The machine's stipulated valuations and compensation in efficient-breach distinguish the buyer's $400 expectation from the seller's gain. The repeat-business, arbitration and transaction-cost discussion is indexed by remedies-in-action. These examples test practical enforcement assumptions. They do not give authority to disgorge all profitable breaches.
