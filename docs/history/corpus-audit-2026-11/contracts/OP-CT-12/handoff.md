## Not applied, for Astra

- **K-21 node d** sends a Swinton-type claim for avoidance to "no avoidance", though Restatement § 161(b),
  Illus. 5 can treat that silence as an assertion. **Node g** omits the peculiar-knowledge exception that
  Psenicska applied. Both are branch-structure changes. See `challenge.md` C-12 and C-13.
- **Citation fixes**: the Borat release is cited as Supp. 34–35 but is on 35–36. A Psenicska Cold Call
  paragraph about the district court is cited to the Second Circuit order. Psenicska appeal brief
  Reasoning, "declined to credit the alleged conduct", misreads the order, which said the conduct, if true,
  did the defendants "no credit" (Supp. 34). See `comparison.md` (b) and `challenge.md` C-21, C-22.
- **Rules & authorities**: Restatement § 21 is attached to all five cases, although nothing assigned cites
  it. §§ 159–172, which slides quote (§§ 161, 168, 169), have no entries for this session.
- **Teaching gaps** (`comparison.md` (c)): scienter is needed in tort but not for rescission, so an
  innocent misrepresentation suffices to avoid (C&M 518); promissory fraud as a doctrine (519–521);
  materiality; the "big boy" no-reliance note (516–517); Laidlaw and investment in knowledge (508–509).
