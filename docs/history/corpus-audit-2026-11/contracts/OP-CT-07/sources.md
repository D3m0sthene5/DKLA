## Results a student would now answer differently

- K-12: a mailed acceptance of a telegraphed offer, overtaken by a revocation, now reaches "Power ended" through new node j (C&M 262; §§ 63, 67).
- K-12: a reader who missed an equally prominent withdrawal of a public offer is bound by it (§ 46).
- K-14 b: an unconditional acceptance that asks a question is an acceptance (§ 61); International Filter entered as an offer seeking a promise is no longer sent to "Acceptance not established".
- K-14 f and new node i: missing § 54(2) notice discharges the offeror's duty after an effective acceptance; an offeror who learns of the performance in time is bound.
- K-14 e and the silence entry: an offeror who says silence will do is bound when the silent offeree intends to accept (§ 69(1)(b)); use of offered property binds (§ 69(2)).
- International Filter everywhere: the no-notice result rests on the order's own "becomes a contract when approved" terms, not on any approval clause.
