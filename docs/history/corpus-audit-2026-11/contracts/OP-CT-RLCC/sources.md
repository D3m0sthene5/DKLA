# OP-CT-RLCC: Restatement of Consumer Contracts, 2019 Tentative Draft (Selections) and 2024 Official Text (Atlas)

`crosswalk.md` compares the two editions section by section, in substance as well as numbering; `mentions.md` lists every student-facing Atlas mention of an RLCC section number and whether the edition matters there.

## What the sources show
- The 2024 Official Text inserts a new § 4 (Interpretation and Construction). Sections 1–3 keep their numbers; the Selections' §§ 4–9 are 2024 §§ 5–10. No section's black letter is identical.
- The syllabus numbers follow the 2024 text. A student reading the Selections finds Class 13's "§§ 8, 9" at §§ 7–8, Class 17's "§ 8" at § 7 and Class 18's "§ 6" at § 5 (the Selections' § 6 is Deception). Class 16's "§ 4" has no Selections counterpart; the Selections' § 4 (Discretionary Obligations) also fits that class, and no Class 16 slide deck was located to settle which the professor means.
- Substantive differences a student would notice: § 2 (2024 puts the burden on the business), § 3 (2024 adds the notice in (a)(3), the "fair and equitable" clause in (c) and the bar in (d)), unconscionability (2024 § 6 adds that a sufficiently high degree of one factor can suffice), affirmations (2024 § 8(c) "unreasonably limit"), parol evidence (2024 § 9 "effect reasonably expected by a consumer").
- The coordinator re-read the 2019 headings for §§ 4–8 in the Selections text and the 2024 § 3(b)–(d) and § 6 sentence in the Official Text before applying.
