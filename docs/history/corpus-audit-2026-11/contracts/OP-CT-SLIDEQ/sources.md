# Slide shorthand: classroom wording kept, legal qualification added

Where a lecture note accurately records the professor's slide but the slide's shorthand drops a condition the assigned law contains, the note keeps the slide's wording and a separate sentence beginning "Legal qualification:" states the condition.

| 11, "Ways to forestall modification" | 12 | No-oral-modification clauses are made effective by statute | § 2-209(2): signed agreement; a clause on a merchant's form must be separately signed by a non-merchant | Selections, UCC § 2-209(2) and cmt. 3 |
| 11, "Duress and good faith" | 13 | Unfair terms are a key element, less relevant if the threat is improper enough | Unfair terms are required only for § 176(2) threats; a § 176(1) threat is improper whatever the terms | Selections, Restatement §§ 175(1), 176 |
| 13, "Terminology and effect of integration" | 9 | A merger clause is relevant but not dispositive | Courts are split; some treat the clause as conclusive (Howard v. Perry; Phelps-Dickson) | C&M 542 |
