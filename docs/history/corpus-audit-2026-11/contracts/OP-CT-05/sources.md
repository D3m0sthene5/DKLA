## Not applied, and why

1. **D & G Stout remand amount (comparison E17; ≈ 4.1 and Cold Call episode 5).** Left as written. The casebook's note 2 says the district court awarded damages equal to "the difference between National's initial offer and the final [sale] price", a total of $394,050 (C&M 143), while the Atlas's itemization (retained property, inventory, receivables, real estate) and "not ... all differences between the headline sale prices" come from 805 F. Supp. 1434, which is not assigned. Both may be true if the court computed the difference net of assets General kept; deciding needs the full opinion.
2. **Cohen learning topic "rest90" (E12).** Applied, but limited to the excerpt: the remand excerpt states Minnesota's test without citing § 90 (C&M 135–136); whether the full opinion cites § 90 was not checked.
3. **Hayes (challenge 19).** The challenger noted that the K-07 b exit for a retirement already planned displays "No recovery" for a problem the casebook leaves open (C&M 128). The edge to g stays, now limited to a promisee who would have left anyway "with no other induced change", and node b's detail asks whether the assurance induced a later forbearance such as not seeking other work (§ 90 ill. 4; Hayes "sought no other employment"). No separate branch was added.
4. **Slide 11's and slide 16's other variations** ("What if both understood the doctrine of consideration?"; "What if she was capable of returning to work?"). The assigned pages give no answer to the first; the second is now reached through K-07 b's injustice edge and node b's detail (Feinberg's age and the impossibility of comparable work, C&M 127–128), with no added text beyond that.
5. **Ricketts Cold Call episode 2, paragraph 1** ("Her new employment fit the independence he had offered"). Kept: it is an inference grounded in the court's words "where she could work or remain idle, as she might choose" (C&M 122), and the episode's cue now asks whether the later job mattered.
6. **Feinberg's bonus figures (E23).** Applied with the Atlas's "$2,000": the extract shows "from $300 in the beginning to 2 ..." and loses the rest, and the word "bonus" is lost too, so the sentence now says "a yearly payment". Confirm on the page image.
7. **Reader G9's second home (unjust-enrichment entry).** The § 371 measure went into the slide 3 lecture note only; the unjust-enrichment entry instead received the unrequested-benefit correction, and the glossary's "restitution" entry already measures by the recipient's gain.
8. **Rules & authorities links.** Neither worker raised an authorities finding for this class, and `tools/v25/authorities.json` was not touched.
9. **K-08 node d (not a Class 5 chart).** Its detail says "An unsolicited performance forced on a recipient despite a feasible opportunity to bargain ordinarily supplies no entitlement." The word "forced" carries the same narrowing corrected on K-06 d (the rule covers any unrequested benefit voluntarily conferred, even one the recipient enjoyed, C&M 145–146), though its bargaining clause states the test. Reported for K-08's owner, not changed.
10. **Prior corrections.** The reliance timeline's `now` sentence and `rest90` step note were set by the 2026-11-17 timeline redesign; both are changed here only to restore the objective "should reasonably expect" standard (C&M 126, 128), because "reliance was expected" invites a subjective reading. No other prior correction is repeated.
