## Not applied, and why

1. French art. 1112, later wording: the node no longer attributes a loss-of-chance exclusion to the assigned text. A sentence on
   the later amended article (reportedly the 2018 ratifying law) was not added, because that amendment was not verified against
   a primary source.
2. Cyberchron ≈ at 4.0 ("a fair share of real overhead costs"): the next sentence of the same paragraph states the
   operating-history and allocation condition, so the plain version was left alone. K-18 node g now carries the condition.
3. Sun Printing map Procedural History, "Special Term ruled for the seller": the excerpt shows only that Special Term's order
   was affirmed (C&M 356). Only "judgment" was changed to "order"; what Special Term itself decided needs the full report.
4. Details from the full opinions that the excerpt does not print (Dixon's dates, HOLA ruling and certification remark;
   Cyberchron's termination-liability caps, Swerdloff and Autotrol; Channel's $25,000, 70,400 square feet and Rule 65(a)(2);
   Sun Printing's April 17, 1923 date; Oglebay's $7.41, $5.00, $5.90 and $7.44 figures): not verified, left unchanged.
5. Slide 10's page reference "(366, 362)": no legal error, so no note was added.
6. Dixon Cold Call episode 4 ("Genuine consideration ending without agreement would not itself require imposition of a new
   mortgage"): accurate as a statement about remedy, so it was left.
7. Oglebay learning topic `interpretation` ("repeated extensions … informed the meaning of continued commitment"): mild and
   framed as interpretation, so it was left; the `course-dealing` topic and K-19 node c carry the corrected evidence of intent.
8. Comparison drafts narrowed rather than applied as written: gap 7 (the p. 360 Problem) is stated as the casebook's question,
   because p. 353 speaks of good faith and reasonable efforts, not "reasonable time"; gap 10 (deal breakers) is stated as the
   casebook's question rather than as advice.
