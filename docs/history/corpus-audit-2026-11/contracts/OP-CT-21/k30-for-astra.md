**Facts entered:** Renner v. Kehl. After rescission for mutual mistake, the buyers claim the $229,649.84 they spent developing the land, including five test wells.

**Path taken:** a → "Reliance position" → c → "Compensatory amount supported" → g.

**Result shown:** g "Award supported compensation or restitution": the development (reliance) costs are awarded.

**Source-supported result:** No reliance award. "[A]bsent proof of breach for fraud or misrepresentation a party who rescinds a contract may not recover consequential damages," which the court used for reliance expenses (C&M 1079 and n.*). The buyers recover the down payment plus the amount by which their efforts enhanced the land's value, less the fair rental value of their occupancy (C&M 1080–1081; Restatement §§ 376, 371(b), 384). Under the Restatement, after avoidance for mistake or discharge for impracticability or frustration there is no breach; restitution is the measure (§§ 158(1), 272(1), 376, 377), and reliance is protected only where those rules "will not avoid injustice" (§§ 158(2), 272(2)).

**Suggested addition to node a detail:** "If the contract was avoided for mistake or discharged for impracticability or frustration, there is no breach: measure restitution (node d; §§ 376, 377). Reliance is protected only where restitution will not avoid injustice (§§ 158(2), 272(2)); Renner (Arizona) denied development costs absent fraud or misrepresentation."
