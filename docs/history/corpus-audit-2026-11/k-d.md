| 01 · Mattei · C&M 102–105 | A land contract exists at signing. Obtaining satisfactory leases conditions the buyer’s duty to close. Varied tenant and lease terms call for his honest business judgment. A change of heart about the price supplies no evidence that the leases disappointed him. In a transaction whose quality can be measured objectively, ask what would satisfy a reasonable person. The court reversed the seller’s judgment and did not set damages. [51 Cal. 2d 119, 121–26](https://law.justia.com/cases/california/supreme-court/2d/51/119.html) |
| 02 · Structural Polymer · C&M 108–112 | Missouri’s requirements rule binds SP even if it has no initial demand. If Zoltek matches a rival’s price, SP must buy covered needs from Zoltek. Bad-faith substitution later would raise breach. The jury found SP performed. The damages theories use different numbers of pounds under one cap on large-tow fiber. [543 F.3d 987, parts I, II.A and III](https://law.justia.com/cases/federal/appellate-courts/ca8/07-2057/072057p-2011-02-25.html) |
| 03 · Wood · C&M 113–115 | From the exclusive agency, Wood’s business organization, contingent pay, monthly accounting, and protection of intellectual property, Cardozo inferred a promise of reasonable efforts. The complaint survived the pleading challenge. Wood still had to establish breach and damages. [222 N.Y. 88, 90–92 (1917)](https://www.nycourts.gov/reporter/archives/wood_lucy.htm) |
| 04 · Wood agreement · SEL 690–691 / PDF 697–698 | Locate the actual exchange. Lucy retained approval, could use fabrics in her business without endorsing them, and reserved listed media rights. Wood received an exclusive channel for covered transactions. He paid procurement expenses from his share and accounted monthly. The initial year and written notice at least 90 days before the end of a term qualify the renewal. Different drafting could change the inference about efforts. |
| 05 · UCC § 2-306 · SEL 344–346 / PDF 351–353 | Under (1) identify whose actual good-faith quantity controls, then compare it with an estimate or, absent one, normal or comparable prior quantities. Comments 1–4 discuss business context, permissible variations, a shutdown, fixed limits and transfer of the enterprise. Under (2) the default duties in an exclusive goods deal run in both directions: seller supplies, buyer promotes. Comment 5 discusses diligence. This is a later comparison with Wood. [Model text](https://www.law.cornell.edu/ucc/2/2-306) |
